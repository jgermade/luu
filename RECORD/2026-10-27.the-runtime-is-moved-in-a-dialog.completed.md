# The runtime is moved in a dialog

Asked from the page's feet, in one message:

> *En los footer del Editor y del Chat, usaría un background más claro, para
> que se vean mejor los botones de los dropup. Lo mismo con los propios
> dropup.*
>
> *El texto 'Terminal', lo llamaría 'Runtime', y su dropup, quizá requiera una
> modal en su lugar, que incluya un checkbox que explique las implicaciones,
> como que ya no tendrá instalado lo que hubiera instalado el modelo. Y usa los
> iconos correspondientes.*

## The problem

**A dropup's face did not read on its foot.** The feet are dark islands
(`data-tone="dark"`) on `--luu-surface`, #232323; a face is `--luu-bg`, #111,
bordered in `--luu-border`, #2c2c2c — a border one shade off the ground it
sits on. The menu it opens is `--luu-raised`, #0e0e0e, darker than the foot
it came from.

**The foot said *Terminal* and moved the session.** The picker beside the
word chooses where the session's commands run — the model's as much as the
terminal's — and the word named only the second. And a move is not free:
whatever the model installed where the session runs now stays there, and a
container the session leaves goes with everything in it. A dropup has a line
per option and nowhere to say that, so a click moved the session and the
packages the model had spent three turns installing were gone without a word.

## The proposal

**The two feet with choices in them are a step lighter**, the editor's and the
chat's: `--luu-foot`, #303030, in `luu.css` beside `--luu-bar`, since it is
luu's own surface and no component's. A dropup's menu is lighter again,
`--luu-menu` (#393939), with its own hover (`--luu-menu-hover`), and its face
is bordered in `--luu-border-strong`. `Dropup` reads the two with the tokens it
used as fallbacks, so it stays self-contained; the model picker's face and menu,
which copy the dropup's rule for rule, follow. The inspector's foot, which
holds no choice, keeps the bars' ground.

**The foot says *Runtime***, and where it says *Terminal* was, a button names
where the session runs, by its runtime's mark and its name — `host` with this
machine, `docker · luu-worker:dev` with Docker's. It opens **a dialog**:

- every place a page may choose, the list the dropup had (`placeOptions`), as
  rows with the mark, where it runs, and the posture and its file under it; a
  runtime this machine lacks is there, off, saying why; the one the session is
  in says *now*;
- choosing another shows what a move does, on a warm ground: a **checkbox,
  *Leave behind what the model installed***, whose line names the two places
  — *its container goes with the move: what the model installed in
  `docker · luu-worker:dev` — packages, tools, anything outside this folder —
  is gone*, or *what the model installed on this machine is not in
  `docker · luu-worker:dev`, which starts from its image* — and under it what
  needs no ticking: the next command runs there, the shell open now ends, and
  what the new place is (the folder at its own path and the image's tools, or
  this machine as the person);
- *Move the session* waits for the box. A refusal from the server — a job open,
  a proposal at the gate — is said in the dialog, which stays.

**Mounted at the page's root**, beside the other dialogs, from a flag in the
terminal's store (`terminal.choosing`): a `<dialog>` takes its CSS variables
from where it sits in the DOM, and inside the foot it would have been a dark
island on a light page.

**The marks come from one place**: `placeMark(where)` in `lib/places.js` —
this machine for `host` and `direct`, the runtime's brand where there is one, a
box where there is none — which the new session's *Runtime* field now uses
too. `placeOptions` gains `where`, the runtime a place runs on whether or not
it is a variant, and `livePlace` is the foot's old reckoning of which option the
live session is, shared with the dialog.

## What it costs

- A click more for a move, and a tick. A move is between jobs and rare; a move
  that loses an hour of installs by accident is not.
- Three tokens in the theme's own layer, and the two faces' borders a shade
  stronger everywhere they are drawn.

## Rejected

- **Keeping the dropup and adding a confirm after it.** Two surfaces for one
  choice, and the confirmation would have been the only place the
  explanation fit — so the choosing should be there too.
- **The checkbox's sentence fixed**, the same whatever the two places. *What
  the model installed does not come along* is true of every move and says
  least about the one that costs most: leaving a container.
- **Lighter faces instead of a lighter foot.** Asked the other way round, and
  a face as light as the foot would have no edge left.

## Still open

- A move is refused while a job is open, as before; the dialog says so after
  the click, not before it.

## Built, the same day

As proposed. Tested: `workspace.spec.js` — the foot reads *Runtime* and
`host` with this machine's mark, and its dialog marks `host` *now* with
nothing to confirm and *Move* off; `terminal.spec.js` (where Docker runs) —
moving to the host and back through the dialog, *Move* off until the box is
ticked and the line naming the container that goes. Seen by hand with the
container places made available: the foot, the dialog, the box, *Move*
enabled only once it is ticked.
