# An engine's own sheet: start it, talk to it, read it

> *El botón de play abre una modal; en esa modal usaría tabs: uno para
> iniciar el modelo, otro para iniciar el modelo en un chat en la propia modal
> y otra pestaña más para el output.*
> *El checkbox 'used', mejor 'Enabled'.*

The sheet play opened
([`engines-installed-and-started`](2026-10-26.engines-installed-and-started.completed.md))
listed the models and had *Start engine* and *Start a chat*, and the second
made a **session** and closed Settings. The ask is the other reading: a chat
*in the sheet*, to see the engine answer on the model before anything is made
of it — and the output beside it, which was a sheet of its own behind another
icon.

## The proposal

**One sheet per engine, three tabs**, opened by play on *Start* and by the
output icon on *Output*:

- **Start** — the models it can load, the busy-port warning and *move to
  port N*, *Start engine*, and *New session* (what *Start a chat* was: a
  session on the engine's provider and the model, Settings closed).
- **Chat** — a conversation with the engine on the model chosen in *Start*.
  `POST /api/engines/{name}/chat` takes `{ model, messages }`, runs `ensure` —
  so the engine is downloaded, started or moved onto that model exactly as a
  session's first turn would — and streams the reply back as NDJSON lines,
  `{"text"}` then `{"done"}` or `{"error"}`. **It is not a session**: no
  system prompt, no tools, no budget, no recording, nothing kept past the
  sheet; it is the engine and the model and nothing of luu's between them, and
  the tab says so. Built from `probe_backend` at the engine's own URL, so a
  provider is not needed for it.
- **Output** — what the engine printed, each line coloured by what it says,
  as the separate sheet had it; the tab counts the lines.

**The switch reads *Enabled***, and a card that is not reads *disabled*:
*used* described what luu does with it, *enabled* is what the key says.

## What it costs

- A route that sends whatever the page typed to a model on this machine.
  Loopback-only and JSON-only like every engine route, and to the engine luu
  starts, at `127.0.0.1` — never a provider's URL, so it cannot be pointed at a
  hosted API with a key in it.
- A chat that measures nothing, in a project where every call is measured.
  The exception is the point: it answers *does this engine answer, on this
  model*, which is a question about the engine and not about context.

## Rejected

- **A session in the sheet.** The chat column is the session's surface, with
  its gate, its tools and its folds; a second one in a modal would be a second
  definition of a turn.

## Built, the same day

As proposed. *Start engine* moves the sheet to *Output*, where the start is
seen; the sheet has no *Close* of its own beside the head's ✕. The output
icon still disables itself while there is nothing printed.

Tested: `settings.spec.js` — play opens the sheet on *Start*, *Start engine*
lands on *Output* with the command first, and the output icon opens it on
*Output*; a found card reads *disabled*; and, last in the file, a stand-in
`llama-server` that answers `/health` and streams a chat as server-sent
events: after *move to port N*, *Chat* sends `hola` to it on the model chosen
under *Start* and shows `echo: hola` as it streams, *Output* has the
stand-in's own line, and *New session* leaves the live session on `fake` and
that model.
