# A landing that looks like an agent

## The question

Jesús, on the landing from
[`an-installer-and-a-landing`](2026-10-23.an-installer-and-a-landing.completed.md):
*dale un repaso a la web, parece una página de una librería más que la de un
agente de IA. Inspírate en internet.* And, on the first redo: *veo mucho
texto, deberían ser más ilustraciones (svg)*, and *el logo ya pone luu, no
hace falta la palabra al lado*.

The page was a light documentation page: a blue accent, six cards of prose, a
quick-start block and the installer's options as a definition list. Nothing on
it showed an agent doing anything.

## What other agents' pages do

Looked at opencode.ai, ampcode.com, aider.chat, warp.dev and Charm's Crush
README. What they share, and what was taken:

- **Dark, and a terminal's vocabulary**: mono micro-labels, `//` kickers,
  "fig." captions. Taken.
- **The product working, above the fold**: a video or a recording of a
  session. Taken as a drawn session rather than a video, see below.
- **A strip of the models and providers it speaks to.** Taken, from the marks
  `web/brands/` already carries.
- **Features as mock UI and diagrams rather than paragraphs.** Taken, after
  the first redo still had too much prose.
- Star counts, testimonials, pricing, a newsletter: not taken. luu has none
  of them to show, and a number invented to fill the slot would be worse than
  the gap.

## What the page is

`pages/index.html`, still one file with its CSS and script inline and nothing
from another host.

- **Dark only.** The previous page followed `prefers-color-scheme`; this one
  is drawn once, in luu's own head colours with the mark's amber, because
  every illustration would otherwise need a second palette checked by eye.
- **The mark alone** in the header and the footer, as `<symbol id="luu">`, the
  same paths as before; the link carries the name for a screen reader.
- **fig. 1, a job played out**: a prompt typed, a draft reading read-only, a
  plan *held until you approve* that is approved, the draft folded, an edit, a
  `curl` denied as not in the plan, `cargo test` passing under Landlock and
  seccomp, the answer, and under it the window's budget bar and prefix reuse
  moving with each step, dropping at the fold. **It is illustrated and says
  so in its caption**; the shapes are luu's (the gate, the fold, a verdict that
  names what held it), the numbers are not a recording. The markup is the last
  frame, so with no script or with reduced motion the whole job is shown at
  once. It pauses while off screen.
- **Each section is a drawing and at most a line.** Inline SVG, drawn with the
  page's tokens:
  - *How a job runs*: four scenes — explore (a lens over a file list),
    plan (an Approve being pressed), act (a terminal inside a shield, one
    command passing and one refused), fold (turns pressed into one summary).
  - *Context*: the prompt's order from stable to fresh, with the cached prefix
    locked at the top and code and the prompt fused last; **prefix reuse at 33%
    against 67%**, which are the measured means from
    [`prefix-reuse-and-block-eviction`](2026-08-27.prefix-reuse-and-block-eviction.completed.md)
    and link to it; turns leaving the window whole; and three definitions lit
    in a parsed repository going into the prompt, **837 of 1024 tokens**, which
    is what `luu select "which file applies Landlock between fork and exec?"
    --select-tokens 1024` answered on this checkout.
  - *Safety*: the model outside three rings — luu's check of the plan, an
    optional container, Landlock and seccomp — with `read /etc/passwd`
    stopped at the first and `cargo test` reaching the process.
  - *Local first*: this machine holding luu and the three engines it starts,
    and a remote API reached only through a pill that reads `remote = true`.
- **The one line is shown twice**, in the hero and in the install card, with a
  copy button that is now an icon. The command breaks after `github.io/` and
  never inside `| sh`. The installer's options are still all on the page, in a
  `<details>` under the first-run commands.

Checked at 375, 768 and 1280 with Playwright: no element wider than the
viewport, nothing in the console, the animation reaching its last frame, and
the copy button answering. The page is 82 KB, 15 KB of it the twelve marks.

## What it costs

- **No light theme.** A visitor whose system is light gets the dark page.
- **The drawings are hand-placed SVG**: a change to what a section says is a
  change to coordinates, not to a sentence.
- **fig. 1 can drift from luu.** It is markup, not a recording, so a change to
  how a verdict or a plan reads does not reach it by itself.

## What was rejected

- **A recorded session as the hero** — a video, or the replay embedded. Either
  is heavier than the rest of the page together, and the replay is one click
  away under `demo/`.
- **A font from a font host.** CI's grep refuses it, and the system stacks are
  enough.
- **The brand marks by URL from `demo/brands/`.** It would tie the landing to
  the demo's layout and leave the page broken when opened from disk. They are
  inlined once, as symbols.

## Still open

- Whether fig. 1 should be generated from a real mock run, so it cannot
