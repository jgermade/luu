# An approval without a plan is not a draft

Row 45 of [`ROADMAP/2026-11-08`](../ROADMAP/2026-11-08/README.md), left open by
[`the-alternation-on-the-page`](2026-09-21.the-alternation-on-the-page.completed.md)
§Still open, twice: *the `!plan` rule reads a pre-alternation approved job as a
draft … the honest fix is the kind the store's `RESTORED_DRAFT` already is.*

## The problem

The page has one rule for which half of the alternation a job is:

```js
const isDraft = job => !!job && !job.plan
```

It reads the absence of a plan as *nobody approved this*. That is true of every
stream `serve` writes today, where `job_approved` always carries the plan. It is
not true of a recording written before
[`narrowing`](2026-08-30.narrowing.completed.md) put the plan on that line:

```
{"type":"task_proposed","task":1,"objective":"…","plan":{…}}
{"type":"task_approved","task":1}
```

Replayed, the store gives the job `plan: message.plan ?? null`, and the menu and
the fold both say *draft 1*: exploration nobody approved, about a job somebody
did.

## Where it reaches, and where it does not

Looked for before fixing, because the row did not say:

- **The page's replay** (`?replay=`, the static twin) feeds raw lines to the
  store. **Reached.**
- **The read API** (`SessionView`, a reload, a resume) parses the line in Rust,
  where `plan` is `#[serde(default)]`: a missing plan becomes `Plan::default()`
  and the job a plan that grants nothing. Not a draft, and not reached by this
  row; a different wrong answer, below.
- **No recording on disk has an approval in it**: nothing under `RECORD/runs/`
  carries `task_approved` or `job_approved`. So the case is real in the format
  and absent from the repository; the test writes its own.

## The proposal

A job says whether it was approved, as a fact the store sets where the approval
is, rather than one inferred from a field that may be missing:

| Where the job comes from | `approved` |
| --- | --- |
| `draft_opened` | `false` |
| `job_approved` / `task_approved`, birth or patch | `true` |
| the read API's `JobView` | `approved_by` set, or a plan |

and the rule becomes `!job.plan && !job.approved`. The plan stays `null` for the
old job: it is unknown, and filling it from the proposal would claim the
person approved exactly what was proposed, which nobody recorded.

This is `RESTORED_DRAFT`'s kind of fix in the sense the record meant: say what a
missing value means at the place it goes missing, once, instead of letting each
reader guess.

## What it costs

Four lines in the store, one in the rule. No wire change: `approved_by` was
already on every `JobView`.

## What was rejected

- **The proposal as the approved plan.** It may well be what was approved, and
  *may well be* is the claim a recording exists to avoid. It stays on the job as
  `proposed`, where it already was.
- **A `can_draft` on the view.** The rule is the page's; the server has no
  `is_draft` to send for a raw replay, which is the surface that was wrong.

## Still open

- **The read API turns a missing plan into an empty one.** For a pre-08-30
  stream the job then reads as *grants nothing*, when it ran under the policy
  file, unnarrowed. Making `JobApproved::plan` an `Option` would say *unknown*;
  it touches every match on the variant, for a stream nobody has on disk.
- **`SessionView`'s pre-16 arm** keeps `plan: None` when the line's plan is
  empty, which would read as unknown for an approved empty plan. `approved_by`
  now keeps the page from calling it a draft; the plan it shows is still wrong.
