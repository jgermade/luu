# The debug panel in cards

The Debug inspector was five uppercase headings over sentences, in one column
that only scrolled. It read as a report, not a panel: the one question a
person brings to it — *how full is the window* — was answered by a stacked bar
whose segments were shares of **what the buckets add up to**, not of the
window, so a turn at 6% of an 8K window drew a bar exactly as full as one at
95%. The sentence under it had the right numbers; the picture did not.

The shape this takes came from a screenshot the owner liked: Claude Code's own
side panel, where *Context* is `11% used · 107.2k / 1M`, a thin bar, and a row
per category with its count and its share, and every section is a card that
folds.

## What changed

- **Six cards that fold**: Turn, Context, Prefix reuse, Tool calls, Compaction,
  Prompt. A card's head is its title and a caret; folded, it also says the card
  in one phrase on the far side — `6% used`, `94% reused`, `1 call · 1 denied`,
  `2 folds · 16 saved`, `40 lines`. Which cards are folded is kept in
  `localStorage` (`luu.debug.folded`, in `lib/prefs.js`), because it is a fact
  about a screen and not about the run. Every card is open until somebody
  folds it: each of them is one of the panels the design says justify the
  page.
- **The budget is a share of the window.** `N% used` is what was sent over the
  limit; the bar's ground is the window and what nothing claimed is left
  undrawn; the legend is one row per bucket — swatch, name, count (`37.2k`),
  share to a tenth (`0.4%` is a block worth seeing in an 8K window). Then
  `free`, and then, muted and with `—` for a share, what the turn sent that is
  counted and not budgeted: the tool round trips and the chat template's
  overhead. Those two were sentences; the sentences are now the rows' titles.
  With no window the shares are of the buckets' total, as before, and the head
  says `window unknown`.
- **Prefix reuse is drawn the same way**: `94% reused · 493 / 525`, the bar, a
  row each for shared and new.
- **The turn is a card like the others**: the picker, the live session's model
  and backend, and *Turn N as it was sent* when reading one. The model is shown
  for the live turn only — a turn read back does not record which model
  answered it, and the live one beside it would be a guess.
- **Calls and folds are lists** with an icon per kind of call (read, list,
  write, command) and the details under the name, wrapped inside the card.

Everything the smoke tests read is where it was: `.turn-picker`, `.reading`,
`.legend li`, `.timeline …`, `.folds li`, and every sentence they match.

## What it cost

A component (`views/inspector/debug/card.html`) and one remembered list. And
the numbers in the rows are compact (`7.2k`) where the panel used to print them
exact: the exact count is each row's title, and the sentence under the rows
still carries `N tokens sent, M of L spoken for` in full.

One bug found on the way and fixed before it landed: switching turns
re-evaluates the prefix rows once more while the new turn's `prefix` is already
`null`, so `view?.prefix.prompt_tokens` threw and jq79 logged it — the
inspector smoke test's "no console errors" caught it. Every access in the panel
is `?.` all the way down now.

## Rejected

- **`<details>` for the fold.** It would fold without a line of script, and it
  keeps the body in the DOM while folded — which for the prompt card is the
  whole prompt, laid out and hidden. `:if` drops it.
- **Folding the cards that are empty by default.** An empty card is one line
  saying why it is empty, which is the panel's own idiom; hiding it would make
  "nothing happened" look like "nothing is measured".
- **Shares of the sent total.** That is what the old bar drew, and it is the
  picture that could not tell a nearly empty window from a full one.

## Still open

- The budget's rows do not open into what each block *is* — the screenshot's
  expandable rows (*MCP tools ›*). The chat's Context dialog already groups the
  prompt by bucket; a row that opened it at its bucket would be the link.
- Three tests in `gate.spec.js` fail on `main` before this change and after it
  alike (a session started on a posture, and the two gate tests that start one
  on `wide`); they are not this change's and are left as they were.

## 2026-10-14: four, not three

The whole live suite, run after the section above was written: 32 pass and 4
fail, and the fourth is `workspace.spec.js` › *the tab shows a changed file's
diff in a modal*. It fails on `main` too, on the same line — waiting for the
terminal's foot to say the session is on the host
(`.state.open[data-place="host"]`) — which is the terminal and not this panel.
So: four tests red before this change and after it, none of them about the
inspector, and every test that reads the inspector green.
