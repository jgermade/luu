# Three columns that each have a footer

The three-pane layout from
[`RECORD/2026-09-15.a-three-pane-inspector.completed.md`](2026-09-15.a-three-pane-inspector.completed.md)
put the right things next to each other and then left every one of them to
invent its own chrome. This is the pass that gives the three columns the same
skeleton — a 40px head, a body that scrolls, a 40px foot — removes the page-wide
`<header>` that was competing with all three, and answers the two questions the
page could not answer at all: *which folder is this* and *is the model I am
about to send to actually there*.

Planned as `WIP`, and `completed` once the code it plans landed — the *As built*
section at the bottom is the difference between the two.

## The problem, in four parts

**1. There is a page header and there are three columns, and they disagree.**
The `<header>` carries the word `luu`, two tags that open Configuration, a
fixtures `<select>`, a status word and a hamburger. None of those five things
belongs to the page: the tags and the status are about *the chat's* session, the
fixtures picker is about the chat's replay, and the hamburger opens preferences
that are about the browser. So the header is a strip of other columns' controls,
and it costs every column 3rem of height to say so. Worse, it is the only thing
on the page with a fixed height, so `.split` is `calc(100vh - 3rem)` — a magic
number that is wrong the moment the header wraps.

**2. The columns have heads that are not the same head.** The inspector has
`.modes` (a tab strip). The viewer has `.viewer-head`, which is *inside*
`content-viewer.html` and is about the selected file rather than about the
column. The chat has `.session-tabs`. Three heads, three heights, three
paddings, and nothing lines up across the page. None of them has a foot at all,
which is why the composer, the gate, the live-job line and the error banner are
all stacked at the bottom of the chat as ordinary flow content and the
transcript's scroll region shrinks every time one appears.

**3. The middle column shows exactly one thing and forgets it.** `showFile` and
`showDiff` overwrite `workspace.selected`. Open a file, look at a diff, and the
file is gone — there is no way back to it that does not go through the tree
again. The column is the one place on the page that is *about* holding several
things at once, and it is the only one with no tabs.

**4. Two questions the page cannot answer.** Which folder am I looking at — the
tree is rooted at the sandbox base and nothing says what that base is, let alone
lets a person narrow it to the subdirectory they are actually working in. And:
is the model this session names actually pulled? `/api/providers/{name}/models`
has been able to answer that since the session starter was built, and the
composer has never asked; a send to a model the provider does not have fails at
the far end of a turn, which is the most expensive place to find out.

## The proposal

### One skeleton, three columns

Every column is `head / main / foot`, and the heads and feet are the same
height — `--chrome-h: 40px`, one token, set once. The page-wide `<header>`
goes, and with it the `calc(100vh - 3rem)`: the grid is `100dvh` and the columns
size themselves.

The chat's foot is the one exception and it is deliberate: **two rows, 80px**,
because the composer is two different questions. The top row is what you are
sending. The bottom row is what you are sending it *under* — manual or automatic
confirmation, which provider and model, and an attachment. Those were not on the
page at all (confirmation), or only inside a modal (provider/model), which meant
the two facts that decide what a prompt will cost were invisible at the moment
somebody typed one.

Where each 40px goes:

| column    | head                                   | foot                                      |
|-----------|----------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------|
| inspector | the `luu` logo, then the panel tabs     | the workspace root, and the button that changes it |
| content   | one tab per open thing                  | the open thing's own facts: path, language, truncation, staged/working-tree |
| chat      | the session's name (editable), then `+` / history / settings | the composer, and the line under it |

The inspector's head is where the logo goes because that is the corner of the
page a logo has always been in, and because the inspector is the column that is
always on screen. **Clicking it switches to the chat** — which is a real
behaviour in the two-column layout and a focus in the three-column one, and in
both it means the same thing: *take me back to the conversation*.

The content column's facts move from a head into the **foot** because the head
is now tabs. This is not a demotion: `path`, `language` and `cut at 512 KB` are
what a status bar is for, and putting them there is what frees the head to carry
more than one file at a time.

### The two layouts, and the breakpoint that is a number

- **`min-width: 1260px`** — three columns, holy grail: inspector, content, chat.
- **`max-width: 1259px`** — two columns: inspector, and then *either* content or
  chat, with a toggle at the right of that column's own head.

1260 rather than the current `64rem`/`48rem` pair because the current pair drops
the *content* column and keeps the chat, and then there is no way to get the
content back. A toggle is strictly better than a disappearance: the same width
shows the same two columns, and which of the two the second one is, is a
person's choice rather than the viewport's.

The old `48rem` breakpoint, which dropped the inspector entirely, goes. A
one-column page is a different design and not one anybody asked for; below 1260
the answer is the toggle.

**And the toggle has one automatic behaviour: opening something in the content
column switches to it.** Clicking a file in the tree while the chat is showing
and having nothing happen is the bug that this rule exists to prevent — the
click already said which column the person wants.

`General → layout` can pin the two-column mode on at any width. Somebody with a
wide screen who wants the chat wide is a legitimate case and the breakpoint
cannot know about it.

### Content tabs

`workspace.tabs` is a list of `{ id, kind, title, path, staged }` and
`workspace.active` names one of them. `showFile(path)` and `showDiff(path,
staged)` open a tab or focus the one already open for that id; the tab's × closes
it; closing the active one falls to its neighbour. `kind` is `file`, `diff` or
`debug` — the third is what lets the context panel put a prompt, a trace or a
tool's output in the middle column instead of in a `pre` inside a 20rem rail,
which is the one thing the debug panel has always been too narrow for.

**A tab holds its identity, not its bytes.** Activating a tab re-fetches. The
measurement in
[`RECORD/2026-09-16.what-the-debug-ui-does-not-need.completed.md`](2026-09-16.what-the-debug-ui-does-not-need.completed.md)
is what makes that defensible: the server answers the largest file in this
repository in 20 ms and a small one in 0.6 ms, and the 271 ms that a file costs
is building the rows — which a cache would *not* save, because the rows are
rebuilt on activation either way. So a cache would hold megabytes of JSON to
save 20 ms of the 291 that switching a tab costs. Not built.

### The folder, chosen under the folder `serve` was started in

The root of the tree becomes a choice, and the choice is bounded by the one rule
that makes it free: **the folder `luu serve` was started in is the ceiling, and
what is chosen is a subdirectory of it**. Nothing widens. `/api/workspace/*`
already resolves every path through `Sandbox::check_path` against that base, and
a chosen root is a prefix the page puts on the paths it asks for — so this is
purely a narrowing of what the page *looks at*, with no new authority and no new
endpoint. A picker that could climb above the base would be a browser control
that moves the sandbox, and that is the one thing the workspace surface has
never been.

On first open, with nothing remembered, the page shows the picker over the
whole window: a tree of the base's subdirectories, `use this folder` on each,
and the base itself as the first row. The choice is remembered in
`localStorage`, keyed by the base's own path, so two servers on one machine do
not inherit each other's answer. The inspector's foot then shows the chosen root
and reopens the picker.

`git-status` is asked once for the whole base and filtered to the root in the
page, rather than gaining a parameter: it is one `git status --porcelain` for a
repository either way, and a second parameter would be a second thing to keep
consistent with the prefix.

### A model that is not there says so before a turn, not after one

When the session's model is known, ask `/api/providers/{profile}/models` and
compare. Three answers and they are not the same answer:

- **the model is in the list** — nothing is shown.
- **the provider answered a list and the model is not in it** — the composer is
  disabled and the foot says the model is not pulled there. This is the case
  worth building: it is a typo or an un-pulled model, it is certain, and today
  it costs a whole turn to discover.
- **the provider did not answer** — the composer is *not* disabled and the foot
  says the provider could not be reached. A provider that is starting up is an
  ordinary state and a page that locked its composer over one would be wrong far
  more often than it was right.

And the case before all three: **no providers at all**. Then the chat column's
body is a placeholder with one button, which opens Settings on *Models*. A chat
box that answers from the mock without saying so is the thing
[`RECORD/2026-09-07.the-first-run-has-no-provider.completed.md`](2026-09-07.the-first-run-has-no-provider.completed.md)
already refused once; this puts the same refusal in the column it is about
rather than in a modal that opens over everything.

### The chat has a name, not a tab

One session is on screen, so a tab strip was answering a question — *which
session* — that the column's head can answer in words, and spending a row on it.
The head becomes the session's **title, editable in place**, and three buttons:
`+` for a new session, a clock for the history of them, and a cog for Settings.
The history is the old tab strip, as a popover: every stored session, its turn
count, switch and delete, with the same arm-then-delete × that the strip had —
deleting a session is still `DELETE /api/sessions/{id}` and still irreversible.

Editable in place needs a server endpoint, because a title is stored:
`PATCH /api/sessions/{id}` with `{ "title": … }`. For the live session it sets
the in-memory view's title and checkpoints; for a stored one it loads, retitles
and saves. It refuses an empty title, because a session with no name is a row
nobody can pick out of the history.

### Settings, as sections down the side

The Configuration modal is one scroll containing two unrelated reports and a
seven-column table. It becomes a modal with a rail: sections on the left, one
section's content on the right — the shape every operating system's settings
window has converged on, for the reason that the sections are not steps.

- **General** — theme (auto / light / dark), editor (this page's viewer /
  Monaco), layout (responsive / always two columns).
- **Models** — what this server resolved, and the providers table. Both, in that
  order, because the first is *where this run sends* and the second is *what the
  next run may be pointed at*, and reading them apart is what
  [`RECORD/2026-09-07.configuring-from-the-browser.completed.md`](2026-09-07.configuring-from-the-browser.completed.md)
  built them to be read as.

**`auto` is offered and is not the default.** Dark stays what a browser with
nothing remembered gets, because every screenshot and every record in this repo
was taken against it and a default that follows the OS would quietly
reinterpret all of them — the same argument `app.css` already opens with.

`auto` is resolved in `prefs.js` from `matchMedia("(prefers-color-scheme:
light)")` and not by a second copy of the light palette under an `@media` block:
`app.css` already knows how to be light, in one rule of forty tokens, and a
duplicated set of forty tokens is a set that drifts. So what reaches the DOM is
still the two states `app.css` defines — the attribute for light, its absence
for dark — and `auto` is a *stored preference* that picks between them, live,
because somebody who switches their machine to night mode should not have to
reload.

### Monaco: a node dependency, not a payload in the tree

The `editor` setting is real, and Monaco is **an npm dependency of
`crates/luu/ui`, gitignored like every other `node_modules`**, excluded from the
`rust_embed` folder so a release binary does not gain several megabytes of an
editor most runs will not open, and served from disk by one route that resolves
under `ui/node_modules/monaco-editor/min/vs` and canonicalises before it reads.
`make install` fetches it when npm is on the PATH and says so when it is not,
exactly as it already does for the VS Code extension.

When it is not installed the setting says so and stays on this page's viewer.
That is the honest state for a checkout that ran `cargo build` and nothing else,
and it is the same shape as `[ui] icon-theme`: the feature waits to be told it
is there rather than shipping a copy of it.

**This does not reopen the measurement.**
[`RECORD/2026-09-16.what-the-debug-ui-does-not-need.completed.md`](2026-09-16.what-the-debug-ui-does-not-need.completed.md)
asked *should Monaco be the viewer* and answered no with numbers: at the size
this panel is asked for, this viewer paints in a third of the time over a
seventh of the bytes, and the whole file stays findable by the browser's own
Ctrl+F. Nothing here disputes that, and nothing here changes the default. What
it adds is that *this viewer cannot edit, fold, or jump to a symbol*, and the
day somebody wants one of those the answer should be a setting rather than a
rewrite. An opt-in that costs nothing when it is off is a different claim from
the one that was rejected.

## What this costs

- **Every smoke selector that names the header, the session tabs or
  `.viewer-head` breaks.** `tests/smoke/gate.spec.js` asserts on `header
  strong`, `header button.tag-btn` and `.session-tabs .tab.new`. They are
  rewritten against the new chrome in the same commit — a spec that is updated
  later is a spec that was red in between.
- **`app.html` grows a fourth and fifth component and sheds the modals.** The
  settings modal, its two sections and the folder picker come out as their own
  files, the way the three panes did.
- **A second grid.** Two layouts is two sets of `grid-template-columns`, and a
  pinned two-column mode makes it three cases rather than two. Contained by
  putting the decision in one `$:` — `columns` is `3` or `2`, computed from the
  width and the setting, and the CSS keys off one attribute.
- **One new write endpoint.** `PATCH /api/sessions/{id}` is the first thing the
  page can change about a stored session other than deleting it. It is a title,
  it is refused empty, and it does not touch a stream.

## What was rejected

- **Keeping the header and shrinking it.** The header's problem is not its
  height, it is that every control on it belongs to a column. A 24px header
  would be the same five misplaced controls.
- **A folder picker that can climb above the sandbox base.** It would move the
  boundary the whole workspace surface is bounded by, from a policy file into a
  click. The base is where `serve` was started; below it is a view, above it is a
  different program.
- **Caching tab contents.** Argued above: it would hold megabytes to save 20 ms
  of a 291 ms operation.
- **Dropping a column at narrow widths (what the page does today).** It answers
  *which two columns* with the viewport instead of with the person, and it gives
  no way back to the one that vanished.
- **`auto` as the default theme.** Argued above.
- **Vendoring Monaco.** Several MB in the tree for an opt-in, and it contradicts
  the rule the icon themes established: name what the machine has, ship nothing.

## Still open

- **The composer's second row names the model but cannot change it.** Changing
  where a session sends is `Continue here` in the session starter, and it is
  there for a reason — every per-turn number is backend-specific. Whether the
  foot should open that dialog or only report is not decided; it reports.
- **`manual` / `auto` confirmation is a page-side switch over an existing
  server behaviour.** Auto means the page answers the gate as soon as a proposal
  arrives, which is a person deciding once instead of per job — not the server
  running unapproved work, and the distinction has to stay visible. Whether it
  should instead be a *posture* is a real question and is not answered here.
- **The debug tab kind is the narrowest possible version.** It carries text the
  context panel already had; a prompt *diff* between two turns in the middle
  column is the obvious next thing and is not built.
- **Attachment.** The composer's second row has the control; what it attaches is
  `--fragment`'s path, and wiring it to a file picker rooted at the chosen
  folder is left for the pass that does it properly.

## As built, 2026-09-16

The plan above is what was intended; this is what the tree got, and the places
the two differ. Appended rather than edited in, for the reason every record in
here is append-only: the differences are the useful part.

### The bug that cost the most: a `$:` that wrote what it read

`settings-models.html` first built its draft of the providers file in a reactive
statement — `$: fresh = (state.providers, redraft())` — because `openSettings`
set the flag that mounts the modal *before* it had fetched anything, so a draft
built once at setup would have been a draft of `null`. That effect reads
`state.providers` and writes `config`, and jq79 gave up on it after 100 passes
with:

```
jq79: an effect re-woke itself 100 times in a row (it writes what it reads);
giving up on it settling
```

which names neither the statement nor the file. It reproduced only on the
*second* mount of that section, which is why the first pass through the page
looked clean.

**The fix is structural, not a guard.** `openSettings` now sets the flag and
nothing else, and the section that draws the data awaits it in its own
`:setup` — which jq79 runs to completion before the template renders, so there
is no window in which the component exists and the data does not. A fetch in the
opener was always a fetch racing the component that draws its result.

This is the second time this page has lost time to an effect that writes what it
reads; the first was the scope variable called `selected` colliding with the DOM
property of that name, recorded in
[`RECORD/2026-09-15.a-three-pane-inspector.completed.md`](2026-09-15.a-three-pane-inspector.completed.md).
**The rule both point at: a reactive statement computes a value and assigns
nothing else.** Fetching, remembering and redrafting belong in `:setup` or in an
event handler.

### Paths stay base-relative; only the display is narrowed

The first version of the folder root gave every path two spellings — one
relative to the base for the server, one relative to the chosen root for the
page — with an `under()` that added the prefix on the way out. That is a second
chance to get the prefix wrong on every call, and it made a tab's `path` mean
something different from the `path` the tree handed it.

What is in the tree: **everything sent to the server is relative to the base,
exactly as the server hands it out.** The root decides where the tree starts and
which of git's changed paths are listed, and `inside()` exists only to shorten
what a row *shows*. One address space.

### `/api/monaco`, because a 404 is not a good question

Asking whether Monaco is installed by probing `/vendor/monaco/loader.js` and
reading the 404 works, and logs a console error on every visit of every checkout
that has no Monaco — which is most of them, and is exactly the complaint
[`RECORD/2026-09-16.what-the-debug-ui-does-not-need.completed.md`](2026-09-16.what-the-debug-ui-does-not-need.completed.md)
made about a `/api/settings` that a static twin does not have. So the question
has a route: `GET /api/monaco` → `{"installed": bool}`. It is also only asked
where the answer is needed — the settings panel, and the viewer once somebody
has chosen Monaco.

### One tab per diff, not one per side

`diffId` first included `staged`, so the foot's *working tree / staged* toggle
opened a second tab with the same filename in it and the strip claimed two files
were open when one was. The id is the path; the side is a property of the tab
that the toggle replaces.

### The first run opens no modal at all

The plan had the providers editor opening itself on an unconfigured server, as
it always has. With the folder picker in front of it that is two dialogs deep on
a first visit, and the outer one cannot be dismissed. The chat column's own
placeholder — which this change added anyway — is a better answer in every way:
it is in the column the message is about, and its one button opens Settings on
Models. The auto-open is gone.

### Smaller things

- `--chrome-h` lives in `app.css` beside the palette rather than in `app.html`'s
  style block: it is a token the whole page is built on, not one component's
  measurement.
- `.note` beside `.caveat`. Every explanatory line under a setting was
  `--warn` yellow, which spends the one colour that is supposed to mean *this
  may not be what you think*. `.caveat` keeps the yellow and now has one user in
  General (Monaco is not installed); everything else is a dim `.note`.
- `workspace.selected` holds a **copy** of the active tab rather than the object
  out of `workspace.tabs`. Putting one object at two paths in a reactive store
  is asking for the identity to change on every read, which is the same class of
  problem as the effect above even though it was not the cause of it.
- The cog was eight long thin spokes on a small circle and read as a sun at
  16px. It has a ring now, and the teeth only break its edge.
- `SessionStore::retitle` is the store's one patch, and the doc comment says why
  it is one: `save` replaces a whole row because it writes a cache of a fold,
  and a title is the one column that is not part of that fold — renaming through
  `save` would rebuild the row from a view and silently drop the `provider` the
  session picker starts from.

### What the tests cover now

`tests/smoke/gate.spec.js` is ten tests, two of them new: one that the content
column keeps a tab per open thing (and that closing the active one lands on its
neighbour), one that the workspace narrows to a subdirectory and remembers it.
The shell test now asserts the skeleton itself — three heads and three feet, five
at 40px and one at 80 — and that the page-wide `<header>` is gone, which is the
kind of thing that comes back by accident. `crates/luu/tests/serve_ws.rs` gains
`a_session_can_be_named_and_keeps_the_name`, which renames the live session and
then the same session once it is stored, and checks both places the title lives.

The CI step that assembles the static site excludes `node_modules`: Monaco is
served from disk by the binary and the static twin has no binary behind it.
