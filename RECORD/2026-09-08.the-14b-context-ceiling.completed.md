# How far the RTX stretches the 14B's context, and what TurboQuant turned out not to be

Follow-on to [`the-rtx-holds-14b`](2026-09-08.the-rtx-holds-14b.completed.md), same
machine, same session. That record fixed `--ctx-size 8192` to match the 7B run for
comparability and left ~5 GB of the 16.3 GB card unused. This is what that
headroom is actually worth, and a detour that changed what was worth measuring.

## The detour: TurboQuant is not a fork away

The ask that started this was "try TurboQuant" — Google DeepMind's ICLR 2026
KV-cache quantization (Walsh-Hadamard + Lloyd-Max, 3-4 bit, claimed 72-78% KV
memory reduction at <10% overhead). Worth being precise about what it quantizes:
**the KV cache at inference time, not the model's weights.** That distinction
matters here because Qwen3.8-27B — the model the question started as, before
narrowing to "stretch the 14B" — doesn't fit this card at Q4 (~17 GB of weights
alone against 16.3 GB VRAM) for a reason no KV-cache trick touches.

Checked before writing any code: the mainline PR (`ggml-org/llama.cpp#21089`,
`tbq3_0`/`tbq4_0`) is **closed**, CPU-only, and was rejected by a maintainer on
the grounds that llama.cpp's *existing* KV-cache quant types already match it
without the added maintenance surface. The alternative was a scatter of
identically-named community forks (`atomicmilkshake/llama-cpp-turboquant`,
`BoFan-tunning/...`, `paperwave/...`) with no verifiable provenance — not run.

**The maintainer's rejection reason was the finding**: `llama-server` already
ships `-ctk`/`-ctv` (`--cache-type-k`/`-v`), `q8_0`/`q4_0`/`q4_1`/`q5_0`/`q5_1`,
CUDA-supported, in the CachyOS package already installed. No fork, no build from
source, no supply-chain question — the thing TurboQuant was reached for already
ships.

## The sweep

Same model, same box as `the-rtx-holds-14b` — `qwen2.5-coder-14b-instruct-q4_k_m`,
`--n-gpu-layers 99`, `--flash-attn on` (required for a quantized V-cache). Each
row loaded, then was hit with a real `luu chat` turn over `--backend openai`
that made the model propose and execute a `run_command` — not just a load check.

| `--cache-type-k/v` | `--ctx-size` | VRAM used / 16 311 MiB | headroom | real turn |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| f16 (default) | 8 192 | 11 121 | 5 190 | ✓ (`the-rtx-holds-14b`) |
| q8_0 | 32 768 | 12 923 | 3 388 | not re-tested |
| q8_0 | 49 152 | 14 635 | 1 676 | ✓ 1765p/116c |
| q8_0 | 57 344 | 15 475 → 15 485 | 836 → 826 | ✓ 3783p/789c |
| q8_0 | 65 536 | — | — | **OOM at load** — `cudaMalloc failed`, wanted 6 528 MiB more than free |
| q4_0 | 65 536 | 13 259 | 3 052 | ✓ 1765p/102c |
| q4_0 | 98 304 | 15 147 → 15 157 | 1 164 → 1 154 | ✓ 1765p/78c |

Weights are ~8 571 MiB regardless of KV type (Q4_K_M is fixed at build time);
every column above is weights plus KV cache plus compute buffers for that
context length.

## What it says

**q4_0 roughly doubles the usable context of q8_0 for the same VRAM**, which is
exactly what halving the bits per KV element predicts — 98K at q4_0 costs about
what 57K costs at q8_0. Both ends of the sweep were run to a real generated
answer, not just a successful load: a context size that loads can still OOM
during generation as compute buffers grow, and neither did here, though both
`57344`/q8_0 and `98304`/q4_0 finished with under 1.2 GB free, which is inside
the range [`the-floor-on-6gb`](2026-09-08.the-floor-on-6gb.completed.md) already
showed moves with the desktop compositor's own VRAM use on this class of
machine. **Neither of those two rows should be treated as a safe default** —
they're the edge, useful for knowing where it is, not for running against.

`49152`/q8_0 and `65536`/q4_0 are the comfortable middle of each type — both
left 2–3.4 GB free and are the pair worth citing as "this card's real ceiling
at 14B," not the tighter ones above them.

## What this does not answer

- **Not answer quality at reduced KV precision.** Every turn above got a
  coherent reply; nothing here scores whether q4_0's approximation changes an
  answer the way `does-the-model-read-it` scored precision for selection. A
  KV-quantization quality probe is a different, unbuilt measurement, closer in
  shape to that record than to this one.
- **Not the exact byte-level ceiling.** The sweep bisected at ~8-16K
  granularity, not the single largest context that fits — deliberately, since
  the two points already found were already inside the risk band the floor
  record identified.
- **Not Qwen3.8 anything.** The detour that started this — whether a Qwen3.8
  MoE fits here — ends where it started: there is no small MoE in that family
  (27B dense or 2.4T-A95B MoE, nothing between), and the 27B doesn't fit this
  card's weight budget regardless of KV-cache type.
