# Settings as a sheet on a phone

**Status:** completed. It was decided and built the same day.

> En mobile me haría falta arreglar la modal de Settings, para poder configurar
> los modelos
>
> La modal la pondría que se abra desde abajo, con el ancho completo, pudiendo
> arrastrar hacia abajo para cerrar. En la parte de abajo, un slider horizontal
> con las secciones.

The person sent a screenshot from a phone (`luu.germade.dev`, Chrome on
Android).

## What was wrong

On the phone, Settings was the desktop dialog shrunk to fit:

- **The sections took half the width.** They were a 12rem column of names
  beside the content.
- **The content got the other half.** *Modelos y proveedores* broke one word
  per line.
- **Models put another split inside that half.** Its providers and their
  models were two columns, so the models' column was about 90px wide.
- **Every form had the same problem.** A `dl.settings` row is a 9rem label
  beside its field, which left the field a third of a phone.

So the one thing the person needed to do, set up a model, could not be done.

## What was built

**1. `<Modal :sheet>`.** A dialog with `sheet` on is a bottom sheet:

- the whole width, 2.5rem short of the top, with rounded top corners;
- it slides up when it opens and down when it closes;
- a grip on its head. **The head drags it.** Past a quarter of its height, or
  a flick (above 0.5 px/ms), it closes from where the finger left it; less
  than that springs back.

Only the head drags, so the body keeps its own scroll, and `touch-action:
none` on the head stops a pull from being read as a scroll. A child modal
(the provider form) is never a sheet. It still comes down out of its parent's
head, and on a phone that parent is the sheet. The owner decides when a
dialog is a sheet, so `Modal` stays free of the page's rules, as everything
in `components/` is.

**2. `<Rail horizontal>`.** The same rail, laid out in a row that scrolls
sideways and snaps. The bar sits under the chosen section instead of beside
it, and the chosen section is scrolled into view.

**3. Settings uses both on a phone.** The phone rule is `web/lib/phone.js`,
taken out of `app.html` so that the slides and the sheet answer the same
question the same way. It includes a phone held sideways
([`a-phone-held-sideways`](2026-11-08.a-phone-held-sideways.completed.md)).

**4. What was squeezed inside it:**

- **`dl.settings`** puts its label over its value on a phone, and inputs and
  selects take the whole width (`app.css`, under the same rule as a media
  query).
- **`provider-models.html`** puts the providers above their models when its
  container is under 34rem. That is a container query on Settings → Models'
  box, not a media query, because the same component is also in the model
  picker and the new session's dialog, at widths of their own. On a phone the
  box is 26rem tall instead of 18.

## Rejected

- **Dragging from anywhere in the sheet**, as some native sheets allow once
  the body is scrolled to its top. That gesture conflicts with the body's own
  scroll and with the sections' sideways scroll. The head is a clear handle,
  and the close button is still there.
- **Making every modal a sheet on a phone.** That was asked of Settings, and
  the other dialogs (the diff, the context, the project) have not been looked
  at on a phone. The prop makes it one word each when they are.
- **Sections as a `<select>`**, which is the cheapest fix. The person asked
  for a row they can slide, and a row shows the neighbouring sections, which
  a select hides.

## Checked

- **`gate.spec.js`, *Settings on a phone*, at 390×844 with touch:**
  - the dialog is a sheet whose box is x 0, width 390, and bottom 844;
  - the rail is horizontal and below the pane;
  - Models chosen from it is drawn more than 380px wide;
  - a 60px pull springs back to where it was;
  - a 400px pull closes it.
- **By eye**, in screenshots at 390×844: every section (Models with the
  provider form over it, General, Engines, Runtimes, Policies, Sessions) and
  844×390. All of them can be read and used. Policies' list heads and
  Sessions' diagram are tight, but not broken.
- **The live smoke suite**, 78 passed. In the full parallel run, *keeps the
  slides* and *the lists are edited in Settings* failed, and both pass alone.
  *The tab shows a changed file's diff in a modal* fails here alone, and it
  fails the same way on `v0.1.13`, before any of this. It waits on a runtime
  this container does not have.

## Still open

- **Not tried on a device.** The pull is driven by the mouse in the test. A
  real finger on Chrome for Android, and Safari's own pull-to-refresh on the
  page under the sheet, still need a phone.
- **The provider form's tabs** (*Connection*, *Models*) cut the second label
  at 390px.
- **Held sideways**, the sheet leaves about 230px for the section. It works,
  but it is cramped.

## Appended 2026-11-10: the short pull was a flick on a fast machine

*Settings on a phone* failed in CI on
[jgermade/luu#77](https://github.com/jgermade/luu/pull/77): after the short
pull the sheet's bottom was at 1306, not 844 — it had closed. The sheet closes
on a flick, its last move faster than 0.5 px/ms, and the test's *short* pull
was eight 7.5 px moves with nothing between them: a flick whenever two land
within 15 ms, which is the machine's speed and not the gesture. Repeated 20
times here, the test as it was failed once, at that line (bottom 1110).

The short pull now pauses 40 ms a step (~0.19 px/ms), a drag on any machine:
20 of 20, and 15 of 15 with two workers. The sheet is unchanged — a quick
flick should close it — and the long pull, past a quarter of the height,
closes it at any speed, so it stays fast.
