# Sessions side by side: several live sessions, each with its own container

**Status:** proposal; nothing of it is built. Roadmap row 14, *concurrency —
`serve` runs one live session at a time*, which has waited on a design
argument since [`state-of-play`](2026-09-09.state-of-play.completed.md).

> Los runners, serían los runtimes en otra máquina, elegibles desde la UI/CLI
>
> Cierto, entonces en realidad ya lo tenemos implementado con las sesiones
> remotas
>
> Podríamos habilitar tener varias sesiones activas, con varios contenedores en
> paralelo, uno para cada sesión

Three questions were put and answered: **a limit and an idle sleep**,
**engines shared, a second local model warned about**, **a folder per
session**.

## What there is today

One live session per `serve`. Choosing another one — new, or from the history
— checkpoints the live one, ends its worker and its container, and builds the
next in its place ([`multi-session-in-serve`](2026-09-04.multi-session-in-serve.completed.md)).
Switching is fast and nothing runs while you look elsewhere: a turn in one
session and a turn in another cannot happen at once.

`App` holds the live session's state beside the process's:

| The session's | The process's |
| --- | --- |
| `session`, `destination`, `agency` (its worker, its container), `posture`, `grants`, `tools_unlimited`, `view`, `stream`, `session_id`, `session_started_at`, `recorder`, `events` | `engines`, `store`, `approvers`, `tasks`, `icons`, `brands`, `postures`, `environments`, the flags |

**What `multi-session-in-serve` rejected, and why it no longer holds.** It
rejected *per-socket virtual sessions* "because `Agency` / `Sandbox` and worker
container processes are currently single-tenant per session lifecycle." Since
then every session builds its own `Agency` — sandbox, worker, container —
through `posture_for` and `finish`, and nothing in it is shared with another.
What is single-tenant now is `App`, and that is a struct, not a constraint.

## The proposal

### 1. `LiveSession`, and a map of them

```
App (the process)                    LiveSession (one per live session)
├─ engines, store, approvers         ├─ session, destination, agency → worker → container
├─ runtimes, tasks, icons, config    ├─ grants, view, stream, recorder, events
└─ live: Map<id, Arc<LiveSession>>   └─ folder, last_active
```

The session's fields move into `LiveSession` unchanged; `App` keeps the
process's and a map. A turn takes its session's locks, never another's, so two
turns run at once on two sessions and each holds its own gate.

### 2. Addressed, not switched

- `/ws?session=<id>` — the protocol, unchanged, carried for one session. A
  socket with no `session` is the most recently used one, which is what every
  client written before this connects to.
- `/api/session/*` → `/api/sessions/<id>/*`: the same routes, under the session
  they act on. The old paths answer for the most recently used one for a
  release, then go.
- `POST /api/sessions` makes a session **beside** the others. Resuming a stored
  one makes it live beside them too. Neither ends anything.
- `DELETE /api/sessions/<id>/live` ends one: checkpoint, end the worker.

### 3. A limit, and sleep

- `[sessions] max-live` (default 4). Making one more than that is refused,
  naming the live ones and the oldest idle one.
- `[sessions] idle-minutes` (default 30). A live session with no turn, no
  proposal waiting and no client for that long is **put to sleep**:
  checkpointed and its worker ended, exactly what a switch does today. It stays
  in the list; opening it resumes it, as the history does now. A session with a
  turn running or a gate open never sleeps.
- What `install` put in a sleeping session's container goes with the container
  ([`a-session-carries-its-policies`](2026-10-30.a-session-carries-its-policies.completed.md)
  §3); waking says so, as a move does.

### 4. Engines: shared, and a second model warned about

`engines::Supervisor` is already the process's. Sessions sending to the same
local model share its engine; it stops when no **live** session sends to it,
where today it stops when *the* session does not. Starting a session on a
second local model is allowed, and the dialog says what is already loaded and
how much memory it holds, before the click.

### 5. A folder per session

Each session has its folder, chosen under `serve`'s root and recorded in its
header; its sandbox base, its tree, its git panel, its repository map and its
selection are that folder's. This is roadmap row 49's *the folder chosen
becomes the session's base rather than a filter*, done for every session at
once. Two live sessions in one folder are allowed, and both are told — two
agents writing one tree is the person's call to make, not luu's to forbid.

### 6. The page

One session on screen at a time, as now. The sessions list marks each live
one: running, waiting at the gate, idle, asleep. Switching attaches the page
to another session and detaches from this one; this one keeps running. A
turn that ends or a gate that opens in a session not on screen is a badge on
its row.

## What it costs

- **The largest change to `serve` so far.** Nearly every handler in
  `serve.rs` reads the live session. Done in steps, each leaving luu working:
  1. `LiveSession` split out, with the map holding exactly one — no change in
     behaviour, every test as it is.
  2. The map, the addressed routes and sockets, `max-live` and sleep.
  3. A folder per session (row 49).
  4. The page: the list, attach and detach, badges.
  5. The engines' warning.
- **Memory and containers grow with live sessions**, which is what the limit
  and sleep are for.
- **The recordings do not change**: a session's stream was always its own.

## Rejected

- **A process per session** behind a router. Each `serve` would load its own
  engines, so two sessions on one model would hold it twice — the opposite of
  §4.
- **Runners on another machine** as a separate feature. A session on another
  host already runs entirely there (`[host.*]`), and side by side it can stay
  live while a local one runs.

## Still open

- Whether one person's two sessions in one folder should share a lock on
  writes, or only be told.
- A client of `/ws` that is not the page (an editor, some day): it addresses a
  session like the page does.

## Appended 2026-10-30: step 1, built

`LiveSession` holds the twelve fields that were the live session's —
`destination`, `session`, `events`, `recorder`, `agency`, `posture`, `grants`,
`tools_unlimited`, `view`, `session_started_at`, `session_id`, `stream` — with
their doc comments, moved as they were. `App` keeps the process's and one
`live: LiveSession`, a plain field rather than the map, so that every access
became `app.live.<field>` with no new lifetimes to reason about: a guard taken
from a field of a temporary `Arc` would not have outlived its statement, and
step 2 is where handlers will take their session as a local.

Done by a script, not by hand: the fields and their comments moved by name,
both constructors (`App::create` and the tests' `App` literal) had their
initialisers moved into `live: LiveSession { … }`, and every `app.<field>`,
`state.app.<field>` and — inside `impl App` only — `self.<field>` was
rewritten, method calls skipped (`app.agency()` and `app.destination()` are
`App`'s methods, and stay). The compiler found the rest: four accesses split
across lines, and `map_rendered` moved into `App` before `live`'s initialiser
borrowed it, which a reorder fixed.

**Verified as no change**: `cargo test --workspace`, clippy, fmt; every live
smoke spec, 75 passing, `terminal.spec`'s contained test skipping without a
`luu-worker:dev` image, and `workspace.spec`'s *diff in a modal* failing as it
did before — at the host shell, with and without this.
