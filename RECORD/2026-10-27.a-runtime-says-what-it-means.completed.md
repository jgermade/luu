# A runtime says what it means

Asked from the new session's dialog, over the *Runtime* field, with the
right half of its row empty:

> *En el espacio de la derecha, añade un texto, que con el mismo alto que el
> contenedor del input, explique las implicaciones de usar uno u otro.*

And then:

> *Añadamos también iconos a los Runtime.*

## The problem

**The field names a place and says nothing about it.** `host` and
`docker · luu-worker:dev` are the two answers to *where do its commands run*,
and the line under the field says only that: *where its commands run*. What
the choice changes — what the commands see, whether they reach the network,
what the terminal panel opens onto — is in `luu-design.md`, *the container*,
and nowhere a person choosing would read it.

**A runtime is a word.** Settings → Runtimes lists five cards by name, and
the field shows the name of the one chosen; nothing looks like Docker.

## The proposal

**Beside the field, what choosing it means, and what the other kind would
mean instead** — one text for the option chosen, since the half-row holds
about three lines:

- `host` — *on this machine, as you: your own tools and files, and its
  terminal is your whole account. A container would see only this folder.*
- a container runtime — *inside `docker · luu-worker:dev`: only this folder
  and the image's tools, and no network unless a list allows it. The host
  would be your whole account.*
- `direct`, which the list does not offer today and a hand-written posture
  can — *on this machine, as you, with nothing holding it: there to test the
  worker without a container, not to work in.*

**As tall as the field's box**, not as the field: the box is what the eye
lines it up with, and the description under the box is the field's own. The
height is `.box`'s, worked out from the same tokens — its padding, its label's
line, its value's line, its border — so a change of scale moves both. Under a
phone's width the row is one column and the text takes the height it needs.

**Each runtime wears a mark**, drawn as the providers' are (`BrandIcon`, a
mask over `currentColor`):

- Docker's and Podman's from Simple Icons 16.36.0, the release `web/brands/`
  already came from, under CC0; nerdctl, containerd's own client, wears
  containerd's.
- **Colima has no mark there, and Apple's `container` is Apple's**, which
  Apple does not allow to be used this way (the README already says so of its
  logo). Both are a box — `#i-box`, the page's own — unless a pack has a mark
  by their names.
- `host` and `direct` are this machine, not a brand: `#i-machine`, a new
  symbol, a screen on a stand.

In Settings → Runtimes, before each card's name; in the new session's field,
at the start of the value's line. `InputContainer` gains a `lead` slot for it,
placed the way its caret is at the other end, and pads the control only when
it has one.

## What it costs

- Three SVGs (`docker`, `podman`, `containerd`) and one symbol.
- A slot on a theme component, unused by every other field.
- The height of the text is a formula copied from another component's CSS.
  It names where it comes from; a change to `.box` that forgets it shows as a
  text a few pixels off, and `gate.spec.js` measures both boxes.

## Rejected

- **Both texts at once**, *host* above *container*. Read side by side they are
  the comparison asked for, and they do not fit a box 52px tall at half the
  dialog's width without going down to a size nothing else in it uses.
- **The text as the field's description.** It is three lines under a field
  whose neighbours have one, and it was asked for on the right.
- **Icons inside the `<select>`'s options.** A native option draws text only;
  a custom listbox for five options would be the one select in the page that
  is not one.

## Still open

- The terminal foot's picker names the same places and wears no mark yet.

## Built, the same day

As proposed. Tested: `gate.spec.js` — the field opens on `host` with this
machine's mark and its text, and the text's box is the field's box to the
pixel, top and height; `settings.spec.js` — Docker's, Podman's and
containerd's marks on their cards, and the box on colima's and `container`'s.
