# The design system, applied to luu

Jesús, the same day the theme was built
([`a-design-system-of-components`](2026-10-21.a-design-system-of-components.completed.md)):
*"aplica el sistema de diseño a luu"*. That record said no screen would move
until someone decided which; this is the decision, and it is *all of them*.

## The problem

Applying it is not swapping imports. The page's colours are not one palette but
four layers, and each would be lost by a naive swap:

- **The palette** in `app.css`: `--bg`, `--panel`, `--line`, `--fg`, `--dim`,
  `--accent` and their light re-pointing.
- **Islands**: under the light theme, the columns' heads and feet, a modal's
  head and the terminal take the *whole dark block*; so do the editor and a
  reply's snippets when their tone is dark.
- **Grounds of their own**: the tree's grey, the chat's darker one with its own
  7:1 inks (`--chat-*`), the dividers.
- **Colours with a meaning**: the context buckets (`--b-*`), Monokai's two
  tones (`--code-*`, `--hl-*`), the terminal's sixteen ANSI colours.

And **a custom property that reads another is resolved where it is declared**:
`--accent: var(--luu-accent)` on `:root` is the root's value everywhere below
it, so the chat's re-pointing and every island would stop working the moment
the old names became aliases of the new ones. Aliases are not an option; the
names have to move.

## The proposal

**Every colour the page draws with comes from the theme, under the theme's
names, and the form controls are the theme's.**

1. **Two files in the theme.** `tokens.css` stays what the components need.
   `luu.css` beside it is the theme's answer for luu's own surfaces: the bars'
   ground, the dividers, the tree's and the chat's grounds and inks, the
   buckets, Monokai, the ANSI colours, the chrome height. A second theme
   replaces both; the demo loads only the first.
2. **The app's names become the theme's**, one for one, in `app.css`, every
   view's scoped style, the components that stay, and `terminal.js`:
   `--panel` → `--luu-surface`, `--line` → `--luu-border`, `--dim` →
   `--luu-fg-muted`, `--warn-bg` → `--luu-warn-soft`, `--b-system` →
   `--luu-bucket-system`, `--hl-keyword` → `--luu-hl-keyword`, and so on. The
   chat column re-points the `--luu-*` names, so it keeps working.
3. **Islands are marked, not listed.** The dark block in `tokens.css` applies
   to `[data-tone="dark"]` as well as to a dark root, so any element can be
   one. The heads, feet, a modal's head and the terminal carry
   `data-tone="dark"`; the content column and a snippet bind it to their tone
   preference. `app.css` no longer knows which surfaces are dark — the markup
   says so, and a theme decides what dark is.
4. **`app.css`'s rules for bare elements** (`button`, `input`, `select`,
   `textarea`) are wrapped in `:where()`, so they weigh nothing against a
   component's scoped style, and they read the theme's shape tokens (radius,
   type size) instead of literals.
5. **The form controls are the theme's.** `TextInput`, `Select`, `Textarea`,
   `Checkbox` and `Segmented` replace `web/components/`'s five, at every use.
   The theme gains what those uses need: a narrow numeric width (`size="num"`),
   a `warn` border, and a class for the inner `<select>` that the smoke tests
   address. `toggle.html` and `form-field.html`, which nothing imports, go too.

**What stays in `web/components/`**: `Modal` (the folio, child sheets, a head
slot — nothing in the theme does those yet), `Rail`, `Dropup`, `SaveButton`,
`Scrollable`, `PathText`. They read the theme's names after step 2, so they
draw with its values; replacing them is a later record.

## What it costs

- A rename across about forty files. Mechanical, and checked by grepping for
  every old name afterwards: none may remain.
- **Visible changes**, on purpose: the theme's border grey, its `Segmented`
  (a pill on a track, not an accent fill), its focus rings. The values were
  taken from the page, so the palette itself does not move.
- Raw `<button>`s stay raw — there are a great many — and keep the page's
  rule, now drawn with the theme's tokens. Moving them onto `Button` is its own
  change.

## Rejected

- **Aliases** (`--accent: var(--luu-accent)`): see the problem; they freeze at
  the root.
- **The island selectors in `luu.css`**, re-declaring the dark palette for
  `.col-head` and the rest: a second copy of every dark value, which is what the
  theme exists to stop.
- **Replacing `Modal` now** with the theme's: it would drop the child sheet and
  the folio, both recorded decisions, for a simpler dialog.

## Still open

- `Button` for the raw buttons; `Modal`, `Rail`, `Dropup` and `SaveButton` into
  the theme.
- Hex literals that remain in views' scoped styles, outside the token system.

## Appended 2026-10-21: done, and what it found

Built as proposed. `app.css` lost its two token blocks and its root greys
(`luu.css` has them), 794 references in 43 files were renamed one for one, and
a grep for every old name afterwards finds none. The islands are
`data-tone="dark"` on the three heads, the three feet, a modal's head and the
terminal, and a binding on the content column and a reply's snippet. The
terminal's sixteen ANSI colours moved into `luu.css`, and `terminal.js` reads
them by their new names. `toggle.html` and `form-field.html` went with the five
that were replaced.

**The theme had to answer two things about living on a page with rules of its
own.** `app.css` dims a disabled control on the element itself, at a weight
chosen to beat any scoped rule; inside a theme `TextInput` that dimmed the input
a second time, and on a `Checkbox` it would have made the invisible native box
show at 0.6 over the drawn one. Each theme component now says what a disabled
native element inside it looks like, and wins the tie by coming later. And a
class: the theme's `TextInput` and `Select` marked a warning border `.warn`,
which is the class the page gives the *line of text* saying why. The provider
form's `.warn` matched two elements and the test that adds a provider failed —
and with it the two later Settings tests that read the provider it adds. It is
`.warned` now.

**Two things the old components had been getting wrong, which the swap fixed
without being asked.** `class="place"` on the old `SelectInput` never reached
the `<select>`, so `select.place` did not exist and two gate tests waited for it
until they timed out: they were among the reds this repository has carried on
main. `selectClass` puts it there, and both pass. And the bare `autofocus` on the
provider form's name field now focuses it — a bare attribute was `""` to the old
component, as the theme's record found.
