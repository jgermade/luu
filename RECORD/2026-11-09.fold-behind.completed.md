# Fold behind: a fourth rung, and a full window that says so

Row 21 of [`ROADMAP/2026-11-08`](../ROADMAP/2026-11-08/README.md), decided in
[`five-decisions`](2026-11-09.five-decisions.WIP.md) §21: the person proposed a
new level of compaction, and the shape argued there is what was built.

## The problem

When the window no longer fit, luu pruned (rules B and C, both off by default)
and then evicted whole turns. An evicted turn leaves no trace in the prompt:
the model goes from *turns 1–6 read the parser and ran the tests* to not
knowing turns 1–6 existed. And the person was told only by greyed turns far
above, never at the turn that cut — the 2026-09-18 stance, *make it visible,
not configurable*, had no shape.

## What was built

**The rung.** `Fold::{Never, Behind}` on `Budget`, between rule C and the
floor in `Context::select`:

```
prune (B, C)  →  fold behind  →  evict
```

- **A line, `Context::behind`**, a ratchet like the floor and the prune line.
  Turns of an open job between the floor and the line are one item,
  `Item::Behind`, rendered as two messages: the person's prompts as typed
  (`[turn 3] …`), and `job::behind_text` — `[turns 3-8 compacted to stay in the
  window] <objective>` over the **same body a closed job's fold writes**
  (`summary_body`, split out of `summary_text` so the two cannot drift): what
  the turns were shown, quoted under the cap, and what their tools reported.
  Never a model call.
- **Moved as little as fits** (`fold_to`), or to the line that leaves the
  window smallest, because a summary can cost more than the turns it replaces —
  `prune_to`'s rule and its reason. Between moves the summary is the same bytes,
  so a prefix cache pays once per move.
- **A closed job's fold is left alone**: `items_from` groups closed jobs first,
  and only the turns of an open job fall behind the line.
- **Eviction still runs after**, for a window the summary does not save; the
  floor lands on item boundaries, so a fold behind is kept or dropped whole.
- **A resume starts the line at 0**, the prune line's reason: it is a fact
  about the last render.

**Where it is set.** `--fold-behind` on `chat` and `serve`; `[resend] fold =
"behind"`; `POST /api/sessions` with `fold`; Settings → Sessions, *Fold*, as
the fourth rule beside repeat, prune and results. **Off by default.** Turning
it off mid-session waits for the next session, with the reason in `waiting` —
prune's rule, because the line is a ratchet and nothing already folded unfolds.

**What it says.**

- `ServerMessage::Compacted { turn, turns, tokens, summary_tokens, counter }`,
  published before `evicted` in the order the selection did them.
  `protocol::VERSION` 13; `record::FORMAT` 28, whose header now names `fold`
  (absent before it, which was `never`).
- `TurnView::compacted` on the turn that folded and `compacted_by` on the turns
  it folded — both halves, as an eviction has.
- **The chat**, at the turn that folded: *turns 1–2 compacted to keep the
  window: 480 → 31 tokens*. On each folded turn: *in the prompt as a summary
  since turn 3* — not struck through, because they are still in the prompt.
- `luu chat` prints `== compacted turns …` beside `== evicted`.

## What it costs

- **What a summary of evidence loses**: the model's own conclusions in prose.
  `RECORD/2026-08-30.the-fold-probe-run.completed.md` measured that cost on a
  closed job's fold. Here it lands on the open job, the one being worked on,
  which is why it is off until measured.
- **The person's prompts are kept whole.** Cheap next to tool output, and the
  one part of a turn that is a fact about what was asked.

## What was rejected

- **A summary written by the model**: a call per fold, and a 7B's paraphrase of
  its own turns in place of what they did.
- **Asking before each cut**: a question per long turn at 8K, which is luu's
  main case.
- **Folding a closed job's turns again**: they already are one summary.

## Still open

- **The run.** Row 38's corpus, at 8192, under `--fold-behind` alone and with
  `--prune-behind`: how often it folds, what the summaries cost, and whether
  turn 10's answer — which needs turn 2's command output — survives a fold
  that kept `run_command — ok` and not the output. Predicted: it does not, and
  that is the cost to read.
- **The page's new-session dialog** does not offer the rule per session; the
  file's default and the API do.
- **The static twin's fixtures** are at an older format, as before; a deploy
  assembled from an older binary is refused by the page.

## What was run

- `context::tests`:
  - `folding_behind_keeps_evidence_where_eviction_kept_nothing`: six turns
    whose tool output is most of their cost; off, the window evicts; on, it
    folds turns 1… and evicts nothing, the summary says `read_file — ok`, the
    prompts are there, the window alternates.
  - `a_fold_behind_is_byte_identical_until_the_line_moves`: asked twice, the
    same bytes and no second fold.
  - `a_fold_behind_leaves_a_closed_job_s_fold_alone`.
  - Against their absence — the step disabled — the second and third fail
    (the first is named outside the filter that was run; it asserts the fold
    directly).
- `api::tests::a_fold_behind_marks_the_turn_that_folded_and_the_turns_it_folded`.
- `serve_ws`, `a_full_window_folds_behind_before_it_evicts_and_says_so`: over
  the socket, `compacted` arrives before any `evicted`, the summary costs less
  than what it replaced, and the read side marks the folded turns.
- `gate.spec.js`, *a fold behind is said at the turn that folded…*: a
  recording replayed through `?replay=`; the line, the marks, nothing struck.
