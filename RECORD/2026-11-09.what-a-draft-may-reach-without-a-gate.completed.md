# What a draft may reach, without a gate

Row 44 of [`ROADMAP/2026-11-08`](../ROADMAP/2026-11-08/README.md), left open by
[`what-an-unapproved-turn-may-reach`](2026-09-21.what-an-unapproved-turn-may-reach.completed.md)
§Still open: *the floor is a session fact and the panel only shows it at the
gate. Most drafting turns happen with no gate up, and nothing says what they may
reach while they run.*

## The problem

The floor is on screen in two places, and neither is there while a draft runs:

- the gate, which is up only when a plan is proposed;
- Permissions → Policies, *A draft, without asking*, which a person has to know
  to open.

Otherwise a person learns the floor by being refused, which is what
`sessions.html`'s own placeholder says: *a draft that tries to write discovers
the floor by being refused*.

## The proposal

The job menu beside the session's name — the `draft` / `plan` chip, on screen
for every turn of an open job — opens on one more item:

```
What it may do
reads . · runs ls · reaches the network · writes only to .tmp · /tmp; anything else is asked
─────────────
Plan this
Close & fold
```

- **A draft** shows the floor, from `settings.floor`, in the gate's words.
- **A job** shows its plan's reads, writes and commands: since row 43, what holds
  a job's turns is its plan and never the floor, so the floor there would be the
  wrong answer.
- A click opens Permissions on *Policies*, where the floor has its own heading.
- The chip's title carries the same line, so hovering is enough.

Nothing is resolved in the page. The floor is the server's, as the gate's
comment insists, and the plan is the job's own as approved.

## What it costs

One menu item and two pure functions in `app.html`. No wire change, no new row
of chrome: the composer's row of buttons was moved into this menu on 2026-10-27
precisely to give the column its height back.

## What was rejected

- **A line under the chat head, always visible.** The most visible answer and the
  most expensive one: a permanent row for a fact that changes only when the
  session's lists do.
- **A badge on the shield button.** It would say *something* without saying
  what, which is the failure the gate's floor line was written to fix.
- **The plan's network as *asked*.** A plan may ask for the network a session
  denies, and the gate says so in a sentence. The menu reads the same
  `settings.posture.network` the gate does and leaves *reaches the network*
  out when the session has none, rather than print a grant that does not exist.

## Still open

- **Nothing measures whether a person opens it**, the same denominator problem
  as row 29.
- **`Floor` is still a third description of a sandbox on the wire**, as
  2026-09-21 noted; this adds a reader, not a description.
