## The verdict

**Folding loses answers, on this tree, at this window, with this model.** Not
one blind spot but two of the three closed-task questions in a row, both
`summary dropped it`. The fourth (19) is a real limitation but not this one's —
it fails identically whether the source ever left the window, so it is evidence
about the model reading `luu.toml`, not about compaction.

The specification is exactly the one flagged as suspect before this ever ran:
the evidence block records what a task *did* — paths, commands, exit lines,
denials — and nothing about what a turn *learned* from a file it read or was
handed. Turn 16 survives because "decide, then render" and the byte-identical
prefix are commitments the summary's own plan-and-file framing happens to gesture
at; turns 17 and 18 ask for the *content* of what was read, and content is
precisely the thing evidence blocks do not carry.

`loude-design.md`'s Tasks section says a closed task's summary stands in for
its turns. On this evidence it stands in for what the task *did*, not for what
it *found*, and those are different claims. That gap, and what the summary
should carry to close it, is not decided here — this record is the measurement,
not the proposal.
