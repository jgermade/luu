# Settings → Models, laid out as a new session

Two asks about the same pane:

> *En Settings > Models, usaría una vista similar a la que usamos en new
> Session. Debajo del listado de proveedores (por fuera), la opción para añadir
> uno más. En el listado de Modelos, arriba donde aparece el proveedor elegido,
> añadimos a su derecha la opción de editarlo, en la SubModal, añadimos una
> pestaña tipo danger zone con la opción de quitar el proveedor.*
>
> *En Settings > Models, quitamos la sección de Destino activo de la sesión.*

## What it was

Two sections. **Destino activo de la sesión**: a card of what this server
resolved for the live session — backend, profile, model, the window and its
source, the counter, the posture, the store — with the resolved sandbox behind
a disclosure. **Providers**: one row per profile, each with its backend, URL,
model, window and transport written out, tags, and *make default*, *use →* and
*edit* at its end; the mock as the first row; *+ provider* pinned to the foot
of the pane. The form's foot carried *Remove*, asked twice.

## What it is

- **The card is gone**, hero sentence included. What it said is still served
  (`GET /api/settings` has every field it drew) and the chat's head and each
  turn say where a session sends; what no surface of the page shows any more
  is the **resolved sandbox text** and the posture's name for the live
  session. That is a loss the ask accepts, and it is written down here so the
  next person who misses it knows where it went.
- **The providers are the new session's two columns**, the same component
  (`provider-models.html`) with `every`: it lists the providers whose engine is
  switched off too, which the pickers leave out, since this is where a provider
  is edited. ✓ is the file's default and its model. A model clicked here opens
  the new session's dialog on it, as *use →* does on the provider's own.
- **The focused provider's actions are over its models**, right of its name —
  a `head` slot the component gained, with a `focus` event to say whose: what
  a session there would start on (URL, model, window), its tags, *make
  default*, *use →*, and an edit icon (`Edit <name>`), which opens the form.
- ***+ provider* is under the list**, outside it, where the next one would go.
- **The mock keeps a row of its own**, above the columns: it is not a profile,
  and *no default in the file* is what choosing it writes.
- **Removing is the form's *Danger zone*** — a third tab on an edit, a red box
  saying what goes (`[provider.<name>]` and its models' entries, and the
  default with it if it was this one), and the same *Remove* asked twice. The
  foot keeps *Cancel*, *Connect* and *Save*.

## Rejected

- **A second two-column component for Settings.** The columns are the same
  question asked of the same file; a second copy would drift the first time
  one of them learned something.
- **The mock as a row inside the columns.** It would have made every caller of
  the component know about a provider that is not in the file.

## Tested

`settings.spec.js`: the provider list as the columns' names, an edit opened
from beside the provider's name over its models, the model it was saved on
over its models, *make default* from there, and *Remove* found only under the
*Danger zone* tab. `gate.spec.js` read the session's posture and sandbox from
the card; it reads them from `/api/settings` now.

## Still open

- Whether the resolved sandbox wants a place on the page again — the
  permissions dialog, which already speaks of what a session may do, is the
  obvious one.
