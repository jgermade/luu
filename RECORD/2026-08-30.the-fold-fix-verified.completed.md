Tasks now costs 80.3% of plain (a 19.7% win), down from the 49.95% win the
pre-fix run measured (18 735 / 37 431). The commit that shipped the fix
estimated *"about a third of the fold's token win goes back, and it still
wins by a third against no fold"* — what actually came back is closer to
three-fifths of the win, leaving about a fifth rather than a third. The
quoted fragments are the reason: `task.rs:1-18` and `luu.toml:1-26` together
run several hundred tokens per closed task, paid at every turn from the fold
onward, not once.

Note the plain-run total here (36 288) differs slightly from the prior
record's (37 431) even though the script and flags are identical — sampling
is pinned but the two runs are still two separate server invocations
(different process, model reloaded fresh), and Ollama's `temperature: 0`
pins the argmax choice, not necessarily bit-identical output across restarts
on this backend. The difference is 3%, on the totals, not on which turns
passed or failed, so it does not touch the verdict below.

## The verdict

**The fix works.** Both closed-task questions that broke in the pre-fix run
now answer correctly in the tasks run, matching plain almost word for word.
The one turn that stayed wrong (19) was wrong identically before and after,
on both sides — it was never a fold signal, as the prior record already
argued.

The cost is real and larger than estimated: folding still wins on tokens,
but by a fifth, not a third, once the quote is paid at this model's answer
lengths. `loude-design.md`'s "real-model figures wait on the next run" is
this run; the design doc is updated alongside this record.
